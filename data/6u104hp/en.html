A work co-designed with Ciiyte. Cover image photographed by 等香鱼.

It is an aluminum-alloy 6/7U 104HP power case, whose design was led by Ciiyte. We began designing and iterating in 2023; the earliest version was bulky, and assembling its stand was highly inconvenient. We spent two years refining it, and only in 2025 did we finally arrive at the current version — one we are at last satisfied with.

Its features include: carbon-fiber side panels with single/dual-case stands; aluminum-alloy 3/4-case stands; a stand system that a single person can quickly assemble and disassemble; an ergonomic handle; ten passive 1/8'' and 1/4'' TRS jacks with built-in converters, with the potential to be made active; five passive 2-6 mults that can be converted into active mults, mixers, S&H, logic, and other functions; a high-power, low-ripple 12-36V wide-input power supply; two XLR ports for gooseneck lights, convertible to USB and the like; a cascadable power output; 75×75 VESA mounting holes; and a high-strength aluminum-alloy body and lid. I was involved, to varying degrees, in testing and designing each of these features.

Our starting point for designing this case was simple: when I first got into Eurorack, I wanted a case that was affordable and good to use, yet the cases on the market were either very expensive or not necessarily good. We studied the designs of cases from various brands, selected the practical and cost-effective solutions among them, and — through a series of trade-offs — settled on its final form.

My deepest thanks to Ciiyte: without him, I could never have used such a fine Eurorack case, and without this case I could not have completed many of my creations and activities. Thanks also to FunnyCoCo, who drafted the engineering documents for this case — it was he who turned our ideas into reality.
